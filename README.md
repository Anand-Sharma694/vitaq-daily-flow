# VITAQ: Your Wellness Coach

Build a Complete D2C Consumer Wellness Platform — “VITAQ”



You are an expert product designer, UX/UI designer, full-stack developer, and startup product architect.



Build a polished, production-style consumer health & wellness web application called VITAQ.



1. PRODUCT CONCEPT



VITAQ is an AI-powered preventive wellness platform designed primarily for urban Indian working professionals aged 22–40 who have sedentary, screen-heavy jobs and struggle with exercise, sleep, movement, stress management, and maintaining healthy daily routines.



The product should NOT diagnose diseases or claim to treat medical conditions.



Its purpose is:



«“Turn everyday lifestyle data into simple, personalized actions that help people build healthier routines.”»



Position VITAQ as:



Your Personal Preventive Wellness Coach



Core philosophy:



Track less. Understand more. Act better.



The product should feel like a premium consumer technology brand rather than a hospital, clinic, or traditional medical website.

22. IMPORTANT PRODUCT SAFETY



This is a wellness product, NOT a medical diagnostic application.



Never tell a user:



“You have diabetes.”



“You have depression.”



“You have hypertension.”



“You have a medical condition.”



Never claim that the wellness score diagnoses disease.



Instead use language such as:



- “Your lifestyle pattern suggests…”

- “You may benefit from…”

- “Consider discussing this with a healthcare professional.”

- “This is general wellness information.”



Include this disclaimer in appropriate places:



«VITAQ provides general wellness information and lifestyle recommendations. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent medical conditions.»
